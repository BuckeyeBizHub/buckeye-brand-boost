# Product: buckeyebizhub.com

Buckeye Biz Hub, LLC is a home-based Columbus, Ohio business (no walk-ins)
run day to day by David Stein. It sells to small businesses in Columbus and
Central Ohio:

- Labels, decals, vehicle lettering and fleet wraps (the categories the
  site leads with). Wraps install through partner shops.
- Business printing, signs and banners (the steady money).
- Spa and clinic launch kits.
- Website, local SEO and small-business consulting.

Visitors are owners and office managers who want a fast, honest quote. The
site's job is to get a call, text or quote form: (614) 561-3358 or
`/contact`. Mode for marketing pages is Persuade; blog posts are Read.

Voice: direct Ohio local business. Short sentences, you/your, real numbers
or none. Prices only from `src/content/prices.ts`.
